# FeedForward

### Turning customer feedback into actionable intelligence

FeedForward is an AI-powered customer feedback intelligence platform designed to transform large volumes of raw customer feedback into structured, prioritized, and actionable insights.

Instead of treating feedback as isolated comments, FeedForward processes customer voices as a continuous stream of intelligence, helping teams identify recurring issues, understand their impact, detect emerging trends, and prioritize meaningful action.

---

## Overview

Modern products receive feedback through multiple channels, but raw feedback alone does not provide a clear picture of what needs attention.

FeedForward addresses this gap through an intelligent pipeline:
